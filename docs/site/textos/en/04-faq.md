## Does VGR replace the police or 190?

No. VGR connects people nearby so they can help, but it does not call the police, an ambulance or the fire brigade. If you are in danger in Brazil, call 190, 192 or 193. <!-- 53 -->

## Do I need an account?

Not to see what is happening nearby, report, offer help, point a direction or use the panic button. You need an account to chat with the person who reported, be rated, receive a reward and become an Authorized Responder. <!-- 32, 34, 35, 51, 169, 180 -->

## Who finds out that I was the one who reported?

Nobody in the app. To answer to the law, VGR keeps a hidden, encrypted record of the internet address used, which can only be opened with a court order or a formal request from the authorities, with the approval of two team members. [Learn more](/en/safety-and-privacy/). <!-- 23, 45 -->

## Without an account, how does the app know the report is mine?

The app keeps a random code on your phone that proves the report was made from it. With it you can chat with helpers, rate them and close the case. If you delete the app or change phones without an account, you lose that control. <!-- 134, 169, 179 -->

## Who sees where it happened?

People nearby see the approximate place: about 110 m, 550 m or 1 km, depending on the case's risk. The exact place is shown only to you and to people who offered help with an account. <!-- 41, 50, 135 -->

## If I help, who finds out who I am?

Nobody, unless you choose to show your name to the person who reported, which is only possible in low- and medium-risk cases. Helpers never see the other helpers. <!-- 55, 237, 238 -->

## Can I help without leaving home?

Yes. Passing information to the authorities, supporting from a distance and spreading the word are also ways to help. None counts more than another. <!-- 5, 10, 208 -->

## Is it safe to go to the place?

Only go if there is no risk to you. VGR never asks anyone to put themselves in danger. <!-- 53 -->

## How does the reward work?

Whoever reports can promise a reward to whoever helps. It is optional and never the reason for helping. During the testing phase, money rewards are switched off. <!-- 1, 30, 247 -->

## What happens when I press the panic button?

The app sends an alert with your position at that moment to the Authorized Responders, people approved by the VGR team. They only see how far away you are, without your name and without your point on a map. Only you can end the alert. <!-- 51, 190, 191, 192, 197 -->

## How do I become an Authorized Responder?

With an account, tap "Become an Authorized Responder". The VGR team reviews the request. <!-- 51, 190 -->

## Do my photos show where and when they were taken?

No, unless you choose to keep that data as evidence. By default it is removed before the photo is stored. <!-- 128, 130 -->

## Can someone on the team read my chats?

Only someone given a specific permission for it, and every read is recorded. Nobody has that permission until it is granted. <!-- 175 -->

## Can I delete a report?

You can close the case. Ninety days after closing, the exact place, the details, the chats and the photos are erased. <!-- 131, 179 -->

## What if someone makes a false report?

A false report can be a crime (articles 339 and 340 of the Brazilian Penal Code) and breaks the [terms of use](/en/terms-of-use/). The team can hide the report, and the hidden record makes it possible to answer to the law. <!-- 23, 57, 161 -->

## Does the app work without internet?

Reports, direction indications and panic alerts are kept on the phone and sent as soon as the connection comes back. <!-- 28, 137, 196 -->

## Where is my data kept?

On a server in Brazil. See the [privacy policy](/en/privacy-policy/). <!-- 240 -->

## How do I delete my account?

See [Delete account and data](/en/delete-account/). <!-- 272 -->
