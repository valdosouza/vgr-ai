## How to ask now

Deleting your account from inside the app is not available yet. Meanwhile, you can ask by e-mail: <!-- 272 -->

- write to **privacidade@[[DOMINIO]]** from the e-mail registered on your account; <!-- 272 -->
- put **Delete account** in the subject; <!-- proposta -->
- we will confirm the request with a code sent to that e-mail and reply within 15 days. <!-- proposta -->

## What happens to your data

- **Your account** (display name, e-mail, password and Google link) is deleted. <!-- 272, proposta -->
- **Your reports and offers of help** are no longer linked to you. <!-- 272, proposta -->
- **What the law requires us to keep** — the hidden accountability record and anything under a court order — is kept for [[PRAZO_A_DEFINIR]] and then deleted. <!-- 23, 141, 272 -->
- **Backups:** whatever is deleted disappears from them within 30 days. <!-- 244 -->

The detail of what is deleted and what is kept will be decided before VGR opens to the public and published here. <!-- 272 -->

## If you used the app without an account

Your reports made without an account are not linked to a name or e-mail. When you delete the app, the code proving they were made from your phone is deleted too. Ninety days after each case is closed, the exact place, the details, the chats and the photos are erased. <!-- 131, 134 -->

## Other requests about your data

Access, correction and the other rights under the LGPD are in the [privacy policy](/en/privacy-policy/). <!-- 267 -->
