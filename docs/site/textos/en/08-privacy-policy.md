## 2. Our principle

People who report and people who help may be at risk if they are identified. That is why we process as little data as possible, show each person only what they need to see, and protect above all the link between a person and a report. <!-- 6, 40, 110 -->

## 3. What data we process

### When you use the app without an account

- **Your position, to show what is Nearby.** It is sent with each request and not stored. <!-- 2, 21, 110 -->
- **A random code kept on your phone**, proving that a report was made from it. It does not identify you. <!-- 134 -->
- **The internet address (IP)** used in actions without identification, in a hidden, encrypted record (see section 6). <!-- 23, 44 -->

### When you create an account

- display name, which you choose and is not verified; <!-- 119 -->
- e-mail, and whether it was confirmed; <!-- 119, 151 -->
- password, stored only in an irreversible form (never the password itself); <!-- 113 -->
- the version of the terms you accepted and when; <!-- 271 -->
- if you sign in with Google: your Google account identifier, e-mail and whether it is verified. We do not store your photo, contacts or other Google data; <!-- 119 -->
- data about your sessions, to keep you signed in safely. <!-- 112, 122 -->

We do not ask for a CPF, date of birth, address, phone number or photo for the account. <!-- 119, 127 -->

### When you report

The kind of case, who or what is involved, the details and any text you write, the phone's exact position at that moment, the date and time it was sent, whether the report is anonymous, and the photos. If you are signed in to your account, the report is linked to it internally, even when you choose anonymity; nobody in the app sees that link. <!-- 3, 9, 23, 32, 135 -->

### Photos

We remove photos' technical data (the camera's place and date, the device model) before storing them, unless you choose to keep it as evidence. We also keep a code that proves the photo was not altered. <!-- 126, 128, 129, 130, 139 -->

### When you help, chat, rate or point a direction

- **Offer of help:** the ways of helping you chose, whether you want to show your name and, if there is one, your account. <!-- 10, 208, 237 -->
- **Chat:** the text of the messages and their times. <!-- 169, 173 -->
- **Rating:** the score given to an offer. <!-- 48, 183 -->
- **Direction:** the direction pointed and, if there is one, your account. <!-- 22, 200 -->

### When you use the panic button

The phone's position at the moment of the alert, the date and time, who received the alert and, if there is one, your account. <!-- 191, 195 -->

### When you ask to be an Authorized Responder

The request, the team's decision and the notes from the review. <!-- 51, 190 -->

### Reward (switched off during the testing phase)

To receive a reward, your registration data (full name, e-mail, CPF or CNPJ, mobile phone, monthly income and address) goes straight to the payment partner and is not kept by VGR, which only keeps the identifier the partner gives you. To reserve a reward, the payer's CPF or CNPJ and name also go to the partner. VGR keeps the amount, the status, the charge identifier and the versions of the notices accepted. <!-- 34, 84, 92, 147 -->

### When a feature is blocked by a legal rule

If you try to use a blocked feature, we record the internet address and, if there is one, your account, to prove that the rule was followed. <!-- 103, 104 -->

### On this site

This site uses no cookies, has no forms and loads nothing from third parties. The server keeps technical access logs for 14 days. <!-- 245, 273, 274 -->

## 4. What we use it for, and on what legal basis

- **Providing the service** (account, reports, help, chat, rating, direction): performance of the contract with you (LGPD, art. 7, V). <!-- proposta -->
- **Panic button:** protection of life and physical safety (art. 7, VII). <!-- 25, proposta -->
- **Hidden accountability record and service security:** compliance with a legal obligation and regular exercise of rights (art. 7, II and VI). <!-- 23, proposta -->
- **Keeping a photo's technical data as evidence:** your consent, given when you choose "Keep" (art. 7, I). <!-- 130, proposta -->
- **Complying with court orders and formal requests from authorities:** compliance with a legal obligation (art. 7, II). <!-- 45, 141, proposta -->
- **Data about children mentioned in reports:** in the child's best interest (art. 14). <!-- 25 -->
- **Statistics:** we only use counts, and no count below 5 is shown. <!-- 166 -->

## 5. Who we share it with

- **Other app users** see only what is necessary: the approximate place and time, without who reported; a helper's name only if they choose. See [Safety and privacy](/en/safety-and-privacy/). <!-- 41, 55, 135, 237 -->
- **Providers that run the service for us:** hosting in Brazil ([[PROVEDOR_SERVIDOR]]), encrypted backups ([[PROVEDOR_BACKUP]]) and e-mail delivery ([[PROVEDOR_EMAIL]]). <!-- 240, 244, 247 -->
- **Google**, only if you sign in with Google. <!-- 119 -->
- **Payment partner** ([[PARCEIRO_PAGAMENTO]]), only when money rewards are switched on and you take part in one. <!-- 59, 84 -->
- **Authorities**, with a court order or formal request, always with the approval of two team members. <!-- 45, 141 -->

We do not sell data and show no ads. <!-- 39 -->

## 6. Anonymity and the hidden record

When someone acts without identifying themselves (reports, helps without an account, chats or rates without an account, points a direction or triggers panic), we keep a record of the internet address used, the kind of action and the time. It is encrypted with a key kept outside the database and does not contain the position. <!-- 23, 44 -->

That record can only be opened on a documented legal basis, such as a court order or a formal request from an authority, or in a formally justified emergency, with the approval of two different team members and a permanent record of every attempt. <!-- 45, 223, 224, 225, 228 -->

## 7. Where it is kept and how we protect it

The data is kept on a server in Brazil. The database and the photos sit on an internal network with no direct internet access. <!-- 240 -->

- The hidden record and each photo are encrypted with their own keys. <!-- 44, 126 -->
- The team accesses a panel with a strong password and two-step verification, and each person only sees what their role allows. The exact place, the photos, the original technical data and the chats require their own permissions, and every look is recorded. <!-- 112, 114, 159, 164, 175 -->
- The most sensitive actions require two different people. <!-- 45, 107, 141, 148 -->
- Backups are encrypted before leaving the server; the key to read them is kept away from it. <!-- 244 -->

If any provider stores data outside Brazil, this will be stated here beforehand. <!-- proposta -->

## 8. How long we keep it

- **Open report:** while the case is open. <!-- 131 -->
- **Closed report:** 90 days after closing, we erase the exact place, the details, the free text and the chats, and the photos can no longer be read. What remains is the kind of case, the dates, the status and, if there is one, the link to the account. <!-- 25, 131, 173 -->
- **Case under a court order:** nothing is erased while it is frozen; the 90 days start again when it is released. <!-- 141 -->
- **Photos that never made it into a report:** 48 hours. <!-- 136 -->
- **E-mail confirmation code:** 15 minutes. <!-- 151 -->
- **App session:** up to 90 days without use. <!-- 122 -->
- **Server technical logs:** 14 days. <!-- 245 -->
- **Backups:** 30 days. Erased data is completely gone within 30 days. <!-- 244 -->
- **Account, offers of help, ratings, direction indications, panic alerts, the hidden record and audit records:** [[PRAZO_A_DEFINIR]]. <!-- 23, 272 -->

## 9. Your rights

Under the LGPD (art. 18), you can ask for: confirmation that we process your data; access to it; correction; anonymization, blocking or deletion of what is unnecessary or processed against the law; portability; information on whom we share it with; deletion of data processed with your consent and withdrawal of consent; and review of decisions made only by automated means. <!-- proposta -->

To ask, write to privacidade@[[DOMINIO]] from your account's e-mail. We reply within 15 days. You can also complain to Brazil's National Data Protection Authority (ANPD). <!-- 267, proposta -->

To delete your account, see [Delete account and data](/en/delete-account/). <!-- 272 -->

## 10. Children and teenagers

VGR is for people aged [[IDADE_MINIMA]] or over. When a report involves a child, the data is processed in the child's best interest. <!-- 25, a decidir -->

## 11. What stays on your phone

The app uses no cookies, trackers or usage analytics. It keeps on the phone only what it needs to work: your session, the code for your reports made without an account, the language you chose and whatever is waiting to be sent without internet. <!-- 11, 28, 134, 255 -->
