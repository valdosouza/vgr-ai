## People looking out for people. Safer communities.

VGR is an app that connects whoever sees something happening with people nearby who can help. You report it in the app. People close by see the report and choose how to help. <!-- 2, 4, 261 -->

## How it works, in three steps

- **Someone reports it.** You say what happened and who or what is involved. The app records where you are. You don't have to identify yourself. <!-- 3, 32, 123 -->
- **People nearby see it.** The report reaches people within a distance that depends on the kind of case: a few kilometres for an assault, more for a missing person. <!-- 2, 7, 29 -->
- **Each person chooses how to help.** Going to the place, passing on information, supporting from a distance, spreading the word or contributing. No way of helping counts more than another. <!-- 5, 10, 208 -->

## Protection comes first

- **Nobody in the app sees who reported.** Anonymity is the default for whoever reports and whoever helps. <!-- 6, 32, 237 -->
- **Helpers decide whether to show their name.** In high-risk cases the name never appears. <!-- 237, 238 -->
- **The place is shown approximately** to outsiders, and more approximately the higher the risk. <!-- 41, 135 -->

[See how we protect you](/en/safety-and-privacy/)

## A reward, when there is one, is optional

Whoever reports can promise a reward to whoever helps. It is never the reason: most help happens without one. During the testing phase, money rewards are switched off. <!-- 1, 30, 247, 262 -->

## A button to ask for help now

In an emergency, the panic button sends an alert to the Authorized Responders, people approved by the VGR team. VGR does not replace the police, ambulance or fire services: if you are in danger in Brazil, call 190, 192 or 193. <!-- 51, 62, 190, 53 -->

## Why VGR

Read out in English, the letters V, G and R sound like "vigiar", Portuguese for keeping watch. For us, keeping watch means looking out for someone: staying alert so that others are safe. VGR also stands for **Vigilance and Reward**. <!-- 250, 252 -->
