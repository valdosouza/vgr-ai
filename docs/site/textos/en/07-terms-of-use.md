## 2. What VGR is and what it is not

VGR is a platform that connects whoever reports that something is happening with people nearby who can help. <!-- 2, 4 -->

VGR **is not an emergency service**. It does not call the police, an ambulance or the fire brigade, does not guarantee that anyone will help and does not guarantee the outcome of any help. If you are in danger in Brazil, call 190, 192 or 193. <!-- 53, proposta -->

Helpers are people from the community who choose to help on their own. They do not work for VGR and do not act on its behalf. <!-- 10, proposta -->

## 5. Using VGR with and without an account

You can see what is happening nearby, report, offer help, point a direction and use the panic button without an account. Without an account, the app keeps a random code on your phone that proves a report was made from it; if you delete the app or change phones, you lose control of that report. <!-- 32, 35, 65, 134, 200 -->

You need an account to chat with the person who reported, be rated, promise or receive a reward and be an Authorized Responder. You create the account with e-mail and password or, on Android, with Google. <!-- 33, 34, 51, 119, 152, 169, 180 -->

You agree to give true information, keep your password to yourself and tell us if you notice your account being misused. The name you choose for your account is not verified and is not shown to other users unless you choose. <!-- 119, 237, proposta -->

## 6. Rules of use

When using VGR, you agree **not** to:

- make a false or bad-faith report. This can be a crime (articles 339 and 340 of the Brazilian Penal Code); <!-- 57 -->
- use VGR to watch, stalk, expose or intimidate anyone. In VGR, keeping watch means looking out for people; <!-- 250, 252 -->
- publish other people's personal data (name, address, phone number, ID) in reports, texts or photos; <!-- 161, 162 -->
- try to pass a phone number, e-mail, website or social media profile in the chat, or get around the block that prevents it; <!-- 171 -->
- harass, threaten or insult anyone; <!-- proposta -->
- pretend to be an authority or a member of the VGR team; <!-- proposta -->
- trigger the panic button without a real emergency; <!-- 62, 196 -->
- try to find out another user's identity or exact location, or get around VGR's security and limits. <!-- 6, 110, proposta -->

## 7. Reports and what you send

You are responsible for what you send. By sending a report, a photo, an offer or a message, you authorize VGR to store that content and show it to other users within the limits of these terms and the [privacy policy](/en/privacy-policy/), always with an approximate place and time for outsiders. <!-- 41, 135, proposta -->

The VGR team can hide a report or block a photo for spam, abuse, illegal content, duplication, exposure of personal data or another recorded reason. The person who reported sees a notice that the report was hidden. <!-- 161, 162, 167 -->

The person who reported can close the case. Ninety days after closing, the exact place, the details, the chats and the photos are erased, unless the case is frozen by a court order. <!-- 131, 141, 179 -->

## 8. Helping

Offering help is voluntary. Never put yourself at risk. VGR does not accompany or supervise help in person and is not responsible for what each person does on site. Respect the law and take to the authorities whatever is for them. <!-- 10, 53, proposta -->

Ticking "Financial contribution" on an offer is an intention. It does not create any payment through VGR. <!-- 10 -->

## 9. Reward

The reward is a **promise of reward** (articles 854 to 860 of the Brazilian Civil Code) made by someone who reports with an account to whoever meets the promised condition. It binds whoever made the promise once the condition is met, can be withdrawn before that and, if several people meet it at the same time, is shared among them. <!-- 30, 33 -->

VGR is not a party to the promise and never holds the money. When money rewards are switched on, the amount is held by a payment partner and is released or refunded according to a mediation carried out by two different team members, with criteria published beforehand and 7 days to contest. <!-- 84, 100, 148, 149, 150 -->

Only someone who helped with an account can receive a reward. <!-- 34 -->

**During the testing phase, money rewards are switched off.** The full rules will be published before they are switched on. <!-- 247, proposta -->

## 10. Panic button and Authorized Responders

The panic button sends a one-off alert, with your position at that moment, to the Authorized Responders approved by the VGR team. They only see the approximate distance, without your name and without your point on a map. Only you can end the alert. The panic button does not call the police. <!-- 51, 53, 190, 191, 192, 197 -->

Anyone with an account can ask to become an Authorized Responder. The VGR team approves or denies the request. Those approved agree to act responsibly, respect the law and never put themselves at risk. <!-- 51, 190, proposta -->

## 11. Anonymity and accountability

Anonymity in VGR protects you from other people, not from the law. When someone acts without identifying themselves, VGR keeps a hidden, encrypted record of the internet address used. That record is only opened on a documented legal basis, such as a court order or a formal request from an authority, or in a formally justified emergency, always with the approval of two different team members. <!-- 23, 44, 45 -->

## 12. Features that depend on a legal rule

Some features are only available when an approved legal rule exists for them in the country. They may become unavailable at any time, and the app tells you when this happens. <!-- 76, 104, 107 -->
